# Pricing — Dabba

Preorder links for home bakers in Pakistan. Customers pay you directly — Dabba never holds money.

## Starter
- Price: Rs 0/month (free during pilot)
- Limits: Up to 20 orders/month
- Features: Order link with menu, photos and prices; sizes, flavours and message on the cake; daily slots and days of notice; advance payment screenshots; ready-written WhatsApp replies

## Baker
- Price: Rs 1,499/month (billed monthly)
- Limits: Up to 200 orders/month
- Features: Everything in Starter; custom cake requests and quotes; live order alerts; special days for Eid and holidays; baking list and recipe costing; customer tracking page

## Bakery
- Price: Rs 3,499/month (billed monthly)
- Limits: Unlimited orders
- Features: Everything in Baker; priority support on WhatsApp; menu set up with you on a call; early access to new features

## Pilot offer
Every baker gets every feature free while we test Dabba. Prices above start after the pilot, with 30 days' notice. No card needed to start. Cancel any time. No commission on orders. Prices exclude tax.
