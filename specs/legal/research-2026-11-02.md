## Scope determination

**Primary jurisdiction:** Pakistan — business is based there and users are mostly there.
**Secondary exposure:** GDPR (EU), UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA (California) — the site is publicly accessible at `dabba.vercel.app`, so if any EU/UK/California residents visit, those frameworks could theoretically apply. Dabba does not target EU/UK/CA users (no localized marketing, no EU/UK/CA currency, no EU/UK/CA language targeting).

## 1. Pakistan — applicable framework

Pakistan does not yet have a comprehensive personal data protection law in force as of 2026. The **Personal Data Protection Bill, 2023** has been drafted but has not been enacted into law. There is no standalone data protection authority.

### Relevant existing laws
- **Pakistan Telecommunication (Re-organization) Act, 1996** and **PECA (Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act), 2016** — cover unauthorized access to data, interception, and misuse of information systems. Relevant to security obligations but not a general data protection regime.
- **Constitution of Pakistan, Article 14** — right to privacy (inviolability of domicile and secrecy of correspondence). Broad constitutional principle, not operationalized for commercial data processing.

### Practical implication for Dabba
- No Pakistani regulator to register with, no data protection officer requirement, no breach notification deadline under a data protection law.
- No lawful-basis framework, no subject-access-request deadline, no erasure right codified in statute.
- Best practice: follow GDPR-lite principles voluntarily (transparency, purpose limitation, security) since no local law governs.

### Primary sources
1. Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (PECA) — Government of Pakistan
2. Personal Data Protection Bill, 2023 (draft, not enacted) — Ministry of IT & Telecom, Pakistan
3. Constitution of Pakistan, Article 14 — right to privacy

## 2. GDPR (EU) — secondary exposure

GDPR applies if Dabba processes personal data of individuals in the EU, or monitors their behavior. Dabba does not target EU users, but the site is publicly accessible.

### Key requirements relevant to Dabba
| Requirement | Relevance to Dabba |
|---|---|
| Lawful basis (Art. 6) | Email sign-in (baker), order data (customer), analytics (consent) |
| Consent (Art. 7) | Analytics consent must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous |
| Controller identification (Art. 13) | Must identify controller, contact details, purposes |
| Right of access (Art. 15) | Customers/bakers can request copy of their data |
| Right to erasure (Art. 17) | "Right to be forgotten" |
| Right to data portability (Art. 20) | Machine-readable copy of their data |
| Breach notification (Art. 33) | 72 hours to supervisory authority |
| Data protection by design (Art. 25) | RLS, consent gating support this |
| International transfers (Art. 44-49) | Data may go to US (Vercel, Supabase, Google) |

### Cookie / consent (ePrivacy Directive)
- Non-essential cookies require prior consent. Dabba's cookie banner gates analytics — consistent with this approach.
- Essential cookies (session, consent preference) do not require consent.

### Primary sources
1. Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) — EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679
2. ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC (as amended) — EUR-Lex
3. European Data Protection Board (EDPB) guidelines — https://edpb.europa.eu/

## 4. CCPA / CPRA (California) — secondary exposure

CCPA applies to for-profit businesses that do business in California and meet thresholds ($25M+ revenue, 100K+ consumers, or 50%+ revenue from selling data). Dabba is a small Pakistan-based business with no California targeting — CCPA likely does not apply. But if it did:

### Key requirements
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete
- Right to opt-out of sale/sharing
- Right to non-discrimination
- Privacy policy must disclose categories collected, purposes, third parties

### Primary sources
1. California Civil Code § 1798.100 et seq. (CCPA) — https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/
2. California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) — https://cppa.ca.gov/

## 5. Third-party service provider obligations

Dabba uses subprocessors. Under GDPR/UK GDPR, the controller must:
- Disclose subprocessors in the privacy policy
- Ensure data processing agreements (DPAs) are in place
- Be liable for subprocessor breaches

Dabba's subprocessors (from code audit):
1. **Vercel** — hosting, edge network, analytics
2. **Supabase** — database, auth, storage (Postgres)
3. **Google** — GA4 analytics
4. **OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation)** — address geocoding

## 6. Specific obligations for Dabba's data flows

| Data flow | Applicable requirement | Practical step |
|---|---|---|
| Baker email sign-in (Supabase Auth) | Lawful basis (contract), identify controller | Privacy policy names controller, contact email |
| Customer name, phone, address (orders) | Purpose limitation, data minimization | Collect only what's needed for order fulfillment |
| Payment screenshots (proofs bucket) | Security, retention | Private bucket, signed URLs, no card data |
| Custom cake photos | Security, retention | Stored in Supabase storage |
| Feedback (rating, message, optional contact) | Purpose limitation | Used for product improvement |
| GA4 + Vercel Analytics | Consent (ePrivacy/GDPR) | Banner gates all analytics; consent stored 1 year |
| Address check via OpenStreetMap | Data minimization | Address text only, name/phone not sent |
| WhatsApp deep links | No data transmitted | `wa.me` links only, no integration |

## 7. What Dabba must include in its privacy policy (minimum)

Given Pakistan has no data protection law and Dabba is pre-attorney with no company email/address, the policy should:

1. **Identify the controller** — with placeholder for name/email/address (do not invent)
2. **Describe what data is collected** — from code audit (baker email, shop profile, customer order data, feedback, analytics)
3. **Describe purposes** — order fulfillment, shop management, analytics (with consent), product improvement
4. **Describe legal basis** — contract (order fulfillment), consent (analytics), legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention)
5. **Describe recipients/subprocessors** — Vercel, Supabase, Google, OpenStreetMap
6. **Describe international transfers** — data may be stored outside Pakistan (US, EU, UK depending on provider)
7. **Describe user rights** — access, correction, deletion (via contact, not self-service)
8. **Describe retention** — honest: no fixed period, data kept while account active
9. **Describe cookies** — analytics cookie, consent banner, 1-year consent duration
10. **Provide contact method** — placeholder (no company email exists)
11. **Supervisory authority** — none in Pakistan; if GDPR applies, relevant EU/UK authority
12. **Children's data** — service not directed at children; users should be 13+
13. **No attorney review** — disclaimer that no lawyer has reviewed

## 8. What Dabba's Terms of Service must include (minimum)

1. **Acceptance of terms** — by using the site
2. **Service description** — preorder platform for home bakers
3. **Baker accounts** — email sign-in, responsibility for shop content

10. **Limitation of liability**
11. **Termination** — baker can close shop; Dabba can suspend accounts
12. **Governing law** — Pakistan (with placeholder)
13. **Contact** — placeholder (no company email)
14. **No attorney review** — disclaimer
